## About Me

I'm Kaiwalya Gondchar — Data Scientist (Product & Growth) at **Vola Finance**, previously with **Indian Navy IDEX** and **Quidich Innovation Labs**.

I build **AI systems that survive contact with production** — agentic fintech copilots for 500K+ users, autonomous USVs for Indian Navy trials, and CUDA-accelerated vision systems for live sports broadcasts.

My edge is where **ML, systems, and product scale** collide: GPS-denied SLAM, air-gapped RAG, BERT-based transaction intelligence, Bedrock agents, and GPU kernels pushed until latency stops complaining.
